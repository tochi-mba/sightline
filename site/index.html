What Sightline is

One app for the camera you already own.

Cheap Wi-Fi action cameras ship with a generic app that tells you to switch off mobile data and does little else well. Sightline replaces it: an Android app, a Windows app and a command line, built on the camera's own protocol, measured on real hardware and written down in the open.

Your internet stays onThe camera joins as a local-only link. Mobile data, your hotspot and USB tethering keep working.
Every settingSightline reads the camera's own menu, so it offers exactly what your camera supports, no more and no less.
The whole cardBrowse photos and videos with thumbnails, play videos straight off the card, download them, and delete them from the camera.
Yours to shapeLayouts, overlays, units, accents and behaviour are all settings, not decisions made for you.

What it does

Everything the camera can do, and more it never could.

Connect without losing the internet

Pick the camera once. On a PC, Sightline uses a spare Wi-Fi adapter and tells you exactly what any choice would cost before it touches a network. It never takes an adapter off your network without asking.

Live view

See the camera's picture as it happens, with grids, crop guides and stream statistics when you want them. Save a snapshot straight from the stream.

Shutter and recording

Take photos and start or stop recording on the camera's card, with the clip timer and the card's remaining time always in view.

Every camera setting

Resolution, exposure, loop recording, white balance, date stamp and the rest, read from the camera itself. Risky ones, like formatting the card, ask first.

The card's photos and videos

Thumbnails, videos that play straight off the card while they are still coming, downloads checked before they are called done, and deletes that only happen once you are sure.

Ride HUD

On Android, a full-screen heads-up display with speed, lean angle and the camera's status in large, steady numerals, with layouts for different rides.

Sentry

Turn the camera into a security camera. Sightline watches for movement in the zones you draw and keeps clips from before and after, even with the screen off.

Updates that look after themselves

Both apps check for new versions and install them when nothing is recording. What changed is shown once, only when something did.

A command line

sightline drives the camera from a terminal: connect, status, settings, photos, recording, snapshots and the card's files, with exit codes scripts can rely on.

Cameras

Will it work with my camera?

Sightline is built for Generalplus-based Wi-Fi action cameras: the ones whose Wi-Fi name starts ActionCam_ and that live at 192.168.100.1 once you join them. A model is listed as supported only once it has been tested and the result dated.

Tested

The reference camera

Generalplus firmware 20240708 V1.3, tested on 2 and 6 October 2026, its quirks included. Its "4K" setting records 1920×1080 at 30 frames a second.

The rule

Evidence before support

A matching Wi-Fi name is a good sign, not proof. Every camera that has been tried, and what worked on it, is in the public acceptance record.

Install

Two minutes, either way.

Sightline runs on Android 10 or newer, and on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

On Android

  1. Download Sightline.apk on the phone. Open this page on the phone and use the download above.
  2. Allow the install. Sightline is not in the Play Store, so Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it for this one install.
  3. Open Sightline and wake the camera's Wi-Fi. The app walks you through connecting. Your mobile data stays on throughout.

On Windows

  1. Run Sightline-Setup.exe. It installs for you alone, needs no administrator, and puts sightline on your path. Prefer nothing installed? Sightline-Portable.exe runs from anywhere.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Sightline is not code-signed yet, and that is the warning Windows shows for every unsigned app.
  3. Plug in a spare Wi-Fi adapter if you have one. It lets the PC stay online through its own Wi-Fi while the adapter talks to the camera. Sightline works with one adapter too and says first what that will mean.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Will my phone lose its internet while it is on the camera?

No. Sightline joins the camera as a local-only connection, so Android keeps using mobile data for everything else. If your phone's only internet is Wi-Fi and it cannot hold two connections at once, Sightline tells you before connecting, not after.

And my PC?

With a spare Wi-Fi adapter, nothing changes: the camera gets the spare and your internet keeps its own. With one adapter, Sightline says exactly what will pause and puts your Wi-Fi back on its network when you disconnect.

Why does Windows warn me before running it?

Because the builds are not code-signed yet. A certificate costs money, and the warning is what Windows shows for every unsigned app. The source is public, and every release lists its files' checksums.

Will another camera work?

If its Wi-Fi name starts ActionCam_ and it answers at 192.168.100.1, very likely. Only tested models are listed as supported; a camera report adds yours.

Why do the downloads sometimes open the release page?

A button becomes a direct download only once GitHub confirms that exact file is on the latest release. If GitHub cannot be reached, the button opens the release page rather than guessing.

Does it cost anything?

No. Sightline is free and MIT licensed, and the source is on GitHub.
